2. Key Features
1) Multi-Counter for Bottle Packing of medications at same time
2) Control of 30 Canisters, Extendable to Max. 90 Canisters.
3) Compact Size for Table-Top
4) Fast-Dispensing within 30 seconds
3. CRETEM MTC-30 Operation Process
①Prescription (order) received from OCS / HIS / 
PMS.
②Put the cup into the position  /
Send the order to MTC.
③MTC starts multiple orders at the same time.
(“Dispensing” displayed on touch panel) 
④Printed out labels at the order in process.
⑤“Wait Bottle Out” is displayed on touch panel when order is completed.
⑥Put out each cup one by one and fill each bottle with tablets.
⑦Order status changed to “Checked by”. Once the user confirms, 
then it can attach the applicable label on each bottle. 
⑧Scan the barcode of completed bottle label 
(order number) for verification by clicking “Checked 
By” on touch panel.
Finally, bottle dispensing for each order is completed.
