## Compliance Packaging

Most individuals are dispensed their prescribed medications in vials or other forms. It may be difficult for some people to reliably follow a schedule for medication as prescribed by a doctor. [1] Compliance packaging is an administration aid that usually contains a week’s supply of medication that is clearly labeled and organizes a patient’s medication schedule into several compartments as prescribed by their doctor.

> * Makes it easier for patients to get all their drugs in labelled pouches rather than in multiple bottles
> * Makes  it easier for patients to take the right drugs at the right time without any difficulties
> * Creates  a consistent patient care by lessening the gap between  prescribing doctors and pharmacists thus eliminating the gap which can worsen  patient care outcomes
> * Able to provide a better patient experience that drives better patient care outcomes
