Smart Tablet Cutter

The tablet cutter provides an efficient and accurate way to split tablets into halves. Built to handle various shapes—such as round, oval, rectangular, and water droplet-shaped tablets—it delivers precise cuts effortlessly. Ideal for hospitals and healthcare facilities, it streamlines the division of solid, regular oral tablets to meet exact dosage requirements.
