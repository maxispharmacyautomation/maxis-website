AdherencePackRx 108

Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging Machine

Experience advanced pharmacy automation with our AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose Compliance Packager. Designed to streamline operations, this cutting-edge solution automates the packaging of multiple medications into a single pouch with unparalleled precision.

Unlike manual systems, this advanced packager features dedicated canisters, each assigned to a specific medication. Once programmed via the intuitive software, the system automatically selects and dispenses the correct medicines from the canisters into the pouch, based on prescribed combinations.

Save time, reduce errors, and enhance efficiency with this high-capacity system. Perfect for pharmacies managing complex regimens, it ensures accuracy, consistency, and superior multi-dose compliance packaging.

How It Works: AdherencePackRx 108 Packer in Action

The AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose compliance Packer packages 40 pouches per minute, with each pouch clearly labeled with the medication name, day of the week, date, and time of administration.


1. Obtain the Drug Bottle
Start by retrieving the prescribed medication bottle and transferring it to the desired canister to begin the packaging process.

2. Select the Drug on Screen
Using the intuitive touchscreen interface, select the medication to be dispensed.

3. Automatically Fill the MTU Slot with the Correct Quantity
The machine automatically transfers the correct combination of pills from canisters into the MTU slot.

4. Automatic Pill Dispensing
Once the MTU slot is loaded, the machine precisely dispenses pills from the MTU into individual multi-dose packaging pouches.

5. Printing Patient & Prescription Details
Each pouch is automatically labeled with essential patient and prescription information, ensuring compliance and traceability.

With the Tabletop AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging system, pharmacies can optimize their workflow and enhance patient adherence through an efficient, automated multi-dose packaging solution.

FAQ

1. What is the packaging speed of the AdherencePackRx108 Multi-Dose Compliance Packager?

Multi-Dose Compliance Packager efficiently produces up to 40 packages per minute, optimizing productivity in medication packaging workflows.

2. What pouch sizes are available?

Multi-Dose Compliance Packager offers two standard pouch sizes: 3.3 inches high by 3 inches wide and 2.2 inches high by 3 inches wide, providing versatility in medication packaging.

3. How does the Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging machine ensure accurate medication dispensing?

The AdherencePackRx108 multi-dose compliance packager features a GS1 barcode scanner that extracts manufacturing date, expiration, and lot number from GS1 barcodes, ensuring accurate tracking and traceability.

4. Is the AdherencePackRx108 compatible with existing pharmacy software systems?

Yes, it integrates seamlessly with various pharmacy software systems, including QS1, ComputerRx, and PioneerRx, facilitating smooth incorporation into existing workflow.

5. What are the dimensions and weight of the  Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging machine?

The machine has a compact design with dimensions of 14 inches in height, 26 inches in length, and 15 inches in depth, and it weighs approximately 60 pounds, making it suitable for countertop placement.

6. What are the power requirements of the  Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging machine?

The AdherencePackRx108 operates on both 115VAC, 60Hz, 320VA, and 230VAC, 50Hz, 320VA power sources, accommodating various electrical standards. 

 7. How does the Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging machine handle labeling and barcode printing?

It supports high-resolution 1D and 2D barcodes (Data Matrix, Aztec, QR, GS1) and allows custom barcodes with multiple data fields, including medication details, pouch packaging date, pharmacy name and address, and patient name, ensuring comprehensive labeling and tracking. 

8. What support services are available?

Maxis Pharmacy Automation Solutions Inc. provides dedicated customer support, including on-site repairs and assistance to quickly resolve any technical issues, ensuring minimal disruption to pharmacy operations. 

9. How does the AdherencePackRx108 benefit growing small and medium-sized retail pharmacies?

Its compact size allows it to fit conveniently on a countertop, reducing space requirements. The machine automates the packaging process, increasing operational efficiency and allowing pharmacy staff to focus more on patient care.
