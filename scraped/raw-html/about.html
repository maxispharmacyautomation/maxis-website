About Us
​At Maxis Pharmacy Automation Solutions, we are committed to redefining the future of pharmacy operations across Canada. As the exclusive Canadian distributor of Unidoses LLC (USA), a leader in pharmacy automation technology, we bring world-class solutions to hospital and institutional pharmacies—focused on safety, efficiency, and innovation.
Headquartered in Ontario, Maxis was founded with a clear mission: to empower pharmacies with cutting-edge automation that improves workflow, reduces errors, and enhances patient care. Our flagship solution, The Smart Pack—Unidoses’ multi-dose pouch packaging and labeling machine—is designed to optimize medication management with precision and reliability.
​
We proudly serve healthcare providers across the country with a focus on:
-> Streamlining medication dispensing processes
-> Supporting compliance with Canadian healthcare standards
-> Minimizing medication errors through intelligent automation
-> Improving pharmacy staff efficiency and workflow
​
Our partnerships are built on trust, transparency, and a shared vision for the future of healthcare. With in-depth knowledge of both the Canadian healthcare landscape and global advancements in pharmacy technology, Maxis is uniquely positioned to bring scalable, cost-effective automation to pharmacies of all sizes.
​
Whether you’re a hospital pharmacy, long-term care facility, or healthcare network looking to improve medication safety and operational performance, Maxis is your trusted partner for innovation-driven solutions.
Let us help you move forward—with precision, performance, and peace of mind.
