Our Products

Smart Pack®

SmartPack® is a tabletop unit-dose packaging system that automates filling, sealing, labeling, and barcode verification for oral solid meds. It handles up to 55 pouches/min, supports four pouch sizes, and includes calibration-free dispensing and pill-orientation tech—ensuring accuracy, compliance, and patient safety.

Learn More

Smart Pack Auto®

Smart Pack Auto® is a fully automated unit-dose packaging system for oral solid meds. Just load bulk tablets into the machine—it fills, seals, labels, and verifies barcoded pouches at up to 55 per minute. With no calibration needed and advanced pill handling, it ensures speed, accuracy, and compliance in every dose.

Learn More

Tabletop AdherencePackRx

TableTop AdherencePackRx is a compact, semi-automatic multi-dose packaging machine. It packs up to 40 pouches/min, each labeled with medication name, dose time, and date. Features include touch-screen selection, calibration-free dispensing, GS1 barcode scanning, pill orientation tech, and dual pouch sizes—enhancing pharmacy efficiency and patient adherence.

Learn More

Adherence Pack Rx 108

AdherencePackRx 108 is a high-capacity Multi-Dose Compliance Packager that automates medication sorting and pouching with precision. Featuring dedicated canisters and smart software, it streamlines operations, reduces errors, and ensures consistent, accurate packaging for complex regimens.
