## How It Works: Tabletop AdherencePackRx in Action

Tabletop Semi-Automatic Multi-Dose Packager Packages 30 pouches per minute with each multi-dose pouch is clearly labeled with the medication name, day of the week, date, and time of administration. 

**1. Obtain the Drug Bottle**

Start by retrieving the prescribed medication bottle to begin the packaging process.

**2. Select the Drug on Screen**

Using the intuitive touchscreen interface, select the medication to be dispensed.

  

**3. Fill the MTU Slot with the Correct Quantity**

Ensure the correct number of pills is placed into the MTU slot for precise dosing.

  

**4. Load Medications Following the On-Screen Guide**

The system provides step-by-step guidance to load medications accurately, minimizing errors.

**5. Automatic Pill Dispensing**

Once loaded, the machine precisely dispenses pills from the MTU into individual multi-dose packaging pouches.

**6. Printing Patient & Prescription Details**

Each pouch is automatically labeled with essential patient and prescription information, ensuring compliance and traceability.

  

With Tabletop AdherencePackRx, pharmacies can optimize their workflow and enhance patient adherence through an efficient, automated multi-dose packaging solution.

## FAQ

## **1. What is the packaging speed of the AdherencePackRx Multi-Dose Packaging Machine?**

The machine packages up to 30 multi-dose pouches per minute, each clearly labeled with the medication name, day of the week, date, and time of administration.

## 2. What pouch sizes are available?

The AdherencePackRx multi-dose packaging machine accommodates two standard pouch sizes: 3.3 inches high by 3 inches wide and 2.2 inches high by 3 inches wide.

## **3. How does the **Multi-Dose Packaging Machine** ensure accurate medication dispensing?**

Multi-dose packaging machine features a built-in barcode scanner for safety, GS1 barcode detection, and a biometric fingerprint scanner to ensure secure and accurate dispensing.

## **4. Is the **Multi-Dose Packaging Machine** compatible with existing pharmacy software systems?**

Yes, it interfaces with various pharmacy software systems, including QS1, ComputerRx, and PioneerRx, facilitating seamless integration into existing workflows.

## 5. What are the dimensions and weight of the **Multi-Dose Packaging Machine**?

The machine has a compact design with dimensions of 32 inches in height, 32 inches in length, and 76 inches in depth, and it weighs approximately 600 pounds.

## 6. What are the power requirements of **Multi-Dose Packaging Machine**?

The AdherencePackRx multi-dose packaging machine operates on 115VAC, 60Hz, 320VA, and 230VAC, 50Hz, 320VA power sources.

## 7. What support services are available?

Maxis Pharmacy Automation Solutions Inc provides 24/7 customer support and offers two onsite service visits per year to ensure optimal performance of the AdherencePackRx multi-dose packaging .

## 8. How does the Multi-Dose Packaging Machine benefit small and medium-sized retail pharmacies?

Its compact size allows it to fit conveniently on a countertop, reducing storage needs. The machine automates the multi-dose packaging process, increasing operational efficiency and allowing pharmacy staff to focus more on patient care.
