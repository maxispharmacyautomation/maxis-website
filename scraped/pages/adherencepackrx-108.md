## AdherencePackRx 108

Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging Machine

Experience advanced pharmacy automation with our AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose Compliance**Packager**. Designed to streamline operations, this cutting-edge solution automates the packaging of multiple medications into a single pouch with unparalleled precision.

Unlike manual systems, this advanced packager features **dedicated canisters**, each assigned to a specific medication. Once programmed via the intuitive software, the system automatically selects and dispenses the correct medicines from the canisters into the pouch, based on prescribed combinations.

Save time, reduce errors, and enhance efficiency with this high-capacity system. Perfect for pharmacies managing complex regimens, it ensures accuracy, consistency, and superior multi-dose compliance packaging.

## How It Works: AdherencePackRx 108 Packer in Action

The AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose compliance Packer packages 40 pouches per minute, with each pouch clearly labeled with the medication name, day of the week, date, and time of administration.

**1. Obtain the Drug Bottle**

Start by retrieving the prescribed medication bottle and transferring it to the desired canister to begin the packaging process.

  

2. Select the Drug on Screen

Using the intuitive touchscreen interface, select the medication to be dispensed.

**3. Automatically Fill the MTU Slot with the Correct Quantity**

The machine automatically transfers the correct combination of pills from canisters into the MTU slot.

**4. Automatic Pill Dispensing**

Once the MTU slot is loaded, the machine precisely dispenses pills from the MTU into individual multi-dose packaging pouches.

**5. Printing Patient & Prescription Details**

Each pouch is automatically labeled with essential patient and prescription information, ensuring compliance and traceability.

  

With the Tabletop AdherencePackRx 108 Multi-Dose Compliance Packaging system, pharmacies can optimize their workflow and enhance patient adherence through an efficient, automated multi-dose packaging solution.
